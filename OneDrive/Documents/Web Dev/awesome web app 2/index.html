Ability Menu

Select an ability from the menu below to bring up its ability card.
Click the cost of the grade you want to add the ability to your cart.
When an ability card lists "Tags" you can click one to open its tag card.
When you add an ability to your cart, you can add tags to it by clicking the tag card's add button.

Click the remove button on the tag card to remove it from the ability in your cart.
To remove an ability from your cart, click its remove button.

Once you have depleted your budget, click the confirm button to continue.
